Questions we get on every call

Ask the crew,
not a call center.

The person who answers the phone is the person who will measure your floor.

How long does a polished floor take?

Plan on 1,000 to 1,500 sq ft per crew per day for a full grind-and-polish. A 5,000 sq ft retail floor is typically four to five working days, and you can walk on it the same day we finish.

Will there be dust everywhere?

No. Every grinder runs tethered to a HEPA dust extractor, and we wet-grind where the space calls for it. Adjacent rooms stay open during the work.

Epoxy or polyaspartic — which one do I want?

Epoxy is the thick, tough body coat. Polyaspartic is the fast-curing, UV-stable topcoat. Most garages get flake epoxy with a polyaspartic top. Sun-facing bays and outdoor patios get polyaspartic all the way through.

Do you test for moisture?

Every slab, before we quote a coating. Moisture is why coatings peel. If the slab reads high we price in a moisture-mitigation primer instead of finding out the hard way.

Can you work while my business stays open?

Yes. Restaurants and retail are usually done overnight or in zones. Tell us your hours and we build the schedule around them.

Is there a warranty?

Every coating system comes with a written warranty against peeling and delamination. Polished concrete doesn't peel, so it carries a workmanship guarantee instead.
